Reporting and blocking

Every post in FiberSocial has a Report action, which sends a flag through Ravelry's own moderation pipeline — the same mechanism you'd get on ravelry.com — so it reaches the group's moderators (and, for more serious issues, Ravelry staff directly).

You can also block a user from a post or their profile. Blocking removes that user's content from your feed immediately, on your device, and keeps them out of your feed and messages going forward. You can review and undo blocks at any time from Settings.

If you'd rather report a concern directly, you can also contact Ravelry or FiberSocial's developer at myhobbyislearning@gmail.com. Suspected child sexual abuse or exploitation material follows the separate process in the Child Safety Standards page.
